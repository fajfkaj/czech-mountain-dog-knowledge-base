Vaughan mushes into Nome
BM V
<p k: i k: A
Above, 94-year-old Norman Vaughan, who in
1928 was the chief dog handler on Richard Byrds
Antarctic expedition, rides a dog team into Nome
on Wednesday, to complete Serum Run 25.
Eleven dog teams and 14 snowmachines began
the journey March 4 in Nenana. The 776-mile
trip, is run to cormrnermorate the historic dog
team relay to bring a shipment of diphtheria
serum to the stricken gold mining town in 1924.
At right, Vaughan hands Nome Mayor Leo
Rasmussen, right, a symbolic package of
dipthera serum.
